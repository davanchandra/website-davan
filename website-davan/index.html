Software Review

A software that I find useful

Visual Studio Code

Visual Studio Code is a code editor made by Microsoft. It can be used to write and edit different programming languages, such as HTML, CSS, Java, and Python.

I think this software is useful for students who are learning programming. The interface is simple, and there are many extensions that can help us when writing code.

One thing I like is that we can change the appearance and add extensions based on our needs. It also supports many programming languages.

However, beginners may need some time to understand all of its features. Overall, I think Visual Studio Code is a good choice for learning and creating projects.

My rating: 4.5/5

Observing A Midsummer Night's Dream

Name: Davan Chandra Kusuma

NIM: 260119010

What Is the Story About?

A Midsummer Night's Dream is a story about love, magic, and funny misunderstandings. The story takes place in Athens and a magical forest.

It tells the story of Hermia, who loves Lysander. However, her father wants her to marry Demetrius. Helena loves Demetrius, but he loves Hermia. Because of this situation, Hermia and Lysander decide to escape into the forest. Demetrius and Helena also follow them.

In the forest, there are fairies named Oberon, Titania, and Puck. Oberon asks Puck to use a magical flower to make Titania fall in love with the first person she sees. Puck also uses the flower on the young lovers, but he makes a mistake. This creates many confusing situations.

There is also a group of workers who are preparing a play for the Duke of Athens' wedding. One of them is Nick Bottom. Puck changes Bottom's head into a gorilla's head, which makes his friends afraid. Titania then falls in love with Bottom because of the magic.

In the end, the magic is fixed, and everything becomes normal again. The couples are happy, and the workers perform their play at the wedding.

My Favorite Character

My favorite character is Nick Bottom. I like him because he is funny, confident, and entertaining. He also really wants to perform well in the play.

I like how he stays confident even when strange things happen to him. His character makes the story more interesting and brings a lot of humor.

My Favorite Scene

My favorite scene is when Puck changes Bottom's head into a gorilla's head during the rehearsal in the forest. This scene is surprising and funny.

His friends are shocked and run away, but Bottom does not understand what happened. He thinks his friends are just playing tricks on him. His confused reaction makes the scene even funnier.

I like this scene because it makes me laugh and shows how magic can create unexpected situations. It is one of the most memorable parts of the story.
