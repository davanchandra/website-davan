Observing a Drama Performance: A Midsummer Night's Dream

Posted by Davan Chandra Kusuma | English Assignment

Last month, I watched a drama performance of A Midsummer Night's Dream at campus. I had never seen a Shakespeare play on stage before, so I was very excited. In this post, I am sharing my observation, and I will also tell you about my favorite character and scene.

What Is the Story About?

The story takes place in Athens and a magical forest. It begins with Hermia, who loves Lysander, but her father wants her to marry Demetrius. Meanwhile, Helena loves Demetrius, but he loves Hermia. Because of this situation, Hermia and Lysander escape to the forest, followed by Demetrius and Helena.

In the forest, the fairies Oberon and Titania have a disagreement. Oberon asks Puck to use a magical flower to make Titania fall in love with the first person she sees. Puck also uses the flower on the young lovers, but he makes a mistake. As a result, the lovers become confused, and their relationships become more complicated. Eventually, the magic is corrected, and the couples find happiness.

At the same time, a group of workers is rehearsing a play for the Duke of Athens' wedding. One of the workers is Nick Bottom. During the rehearsal, Puck changes Bottom's head into a gorilla's head. His friends become frightened and run away. Because of the magic, Titania also falls in love with Bottom. Later, the magical spell is removed, and everything returns to normal.

At the end of the story, the lovers are happy, Oberon and Titania make peace, and the workers perform their play at the wedding celebration. The story shows how love, magic, and misunderstandings can lead to unexpected and funny events.

Favorite and Observed Character: Bottom

My favorite character is Bottom because he is funny, confident, and entertaining. Even though he is not the smartest character, he always believes in himself and wants to perform well. His confidence makes him stand out from the other characters.

I especially like the moment when he gets a gorilla's head but continues acting as if nothing unusual has happened. His behavior makes the audience laugh because he does not understand why everyone is afraid of him. I also admire his enthusiasm for acting and his willingness to take on different roles.

Overall, I like Bottom because he brings humor and excitement to the drama. His unusual transformation makes the story more interesting and memorable. For me, Bottom is the funniest character in A Midsummer Night's Dream.

Favorite Scene

My favorite scene is when Bottom's head is transformed into a gorilla's head during the rehearsal in the forest. I like this scene because it is surprising, funny, and entertaining. When Puck uses his magic, Bottom's appearance suddenly changes, and his friends are shocked by what they see. They immediately run away because they are frightened of him.

What makes this scene even more interesting is Bottom's reaction. He does not realize that his head has changed into a gorilla's head. Instead, he believes that his friends are playing a trick on him. His confused reaction makes the scene more humorous and enjoyable to watch.
